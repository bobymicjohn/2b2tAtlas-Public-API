When consuming or redistributing Atlas records:

3. Review the original source's terms before republishing media files.
4. Distinguish factual metadata from a third party's creative work.
5. Do not represent a historical coordinate as proof of a location's current status.

Questions about a specific Atlas record should include its `apiUrl` or canonical entity URL.
